What's Really In Your Palm Beach Water?

Municipal and well water in South Florida contains a cocktail of contaminants. Our free in-home test shows you exactly what you're dealing with — and our RO systems remove virtually all of it.

Chlorine & Chloramines

City water is treated with chlorine and chloramines — leaving a chemical taste and odor throughout your home. RO removes both completely.

PFAS "Forever Chemicals"

Detected in dozens of Florida water supplies. PFAS don't break down naturally — but reverse osmosis membranes filter them out at the point of use.

Lead & Heavy Metals

Old pipes and solder can leach lead, copper, and arsenic into your drinking water. RO filtration removes heavy metals down to trace levels.

Iron & Sulfur (Well Water)

Well water in Palm Beach County commonly contains iron that stains fixtures orange and sulfur that smells like rotten eggs. We have targeted solutions for both.

Nitrates & Bacteria

Agricultural runoff and aging infrastructure can introduce nitrates and bacteria into the water supply. Multi-stage RO systems include UV purification for complete protection.

Calcium & Minerals (Hard Water)

Palm Beach County water rates 150–300 ppm hardness. Whole-house filtration paired with a softener delivers truly clean, soft water from every tap.
