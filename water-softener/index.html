Is Hard Water Damaging Your Home?

Palm Beach County water hardness of 150–300 ppm causes real, costly problems throughout your home.

Appliances Fail Early

Scale buildup cuts water heater life in half and wears out dishwashers, washing machines, and ice makers years ahead of schedule.

White Scale on Everything

Chalky mineral buildup on faucets, shower heads, tile, and glass that scrubbing can't fully remove.

Dry Skin & Brittle Hair

Hard minerals strip natural oils from skin and hair — leaving you rough and dry no matter how much moisturizer you use.

Spotted Dishes & Glasses

Hard water leaves white spots and cloudy film on dishes and glassware even after the dishwasher.

Pipes Scale and Clog

Calcium buildup inside pipes gradually reduces water pressure and flow throughout your home.

Soap Won't Lather

Hard minerals block proper soap lather — you end up using 2x as much soap, shampoo, and laundry detergent.
