Is Hard Water Destroying Your Home?

Palm Beach County water tests at 150–300 ppm hardness. Without a water softener, this is what happens:

Appliances Die Early

Hard water scale cuts water heater life in half and clogs dishwashers, washing machines, and coffee makers.

White Scale Everywhere

Chalky buildup on faucets, shower heads, tile grout, and glass surfaces that never wipes fully clean.

Dry Skin & Brittle Hair

Hard water strips natural oils from your skin and hair. You feel rough and dry no matter how much moisturizer you use.

Spotted Dishes & Glassware

Even after the dishwasher, hard water leaves white spots and cloudy film on dishes, glasses, and silverware.

Pipes Scale & Restrict

Over time, calcium builds up inside pipes — reducing water flow and pressure throughout your home.

Soap Won't Lather

Hard minerals react with soap and prevent proper lather — you use twice as much soap, shampoo, and detergent.
