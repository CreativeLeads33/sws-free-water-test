Does Your Water Have These Problems?

South Florida water is notoriously hard and full of contaminants. Our free test identifies exactly what's in yours.

Orange & Rust Stains

Iron in your water leaves permanent orange stains in sinks, tubs, toilets, and shower grout.

Hard Water Scale

White chalky buildup on faucets, shower heads, and inside appliances that won't scrub off.

Sulfur / Rotten Egg Smell

Well water in Palm Beach County often smells like rotten eggs, especially when the water is heated.

Dry Skin & Brittle Hair

Hard water strips moisture from your skin and hair, leaving both rough and dry.

Bad-Tasting Drinking Water

Chlorine and dissolved minerals from city water make tap water taste and smell unpleasant.

Appliance Damage

Hard water shortens the life of your water heater, dishwasher, and washing machine.
