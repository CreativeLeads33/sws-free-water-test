South Florida Filtration Problems Are Real

Palm Beach County tap water often contains chlorine, chemicals, and hidden contaminants. Here's what homeowners deal with every day:

Chemical Taste & Smell

Chlorine and chloramines in city water leave your tap water tasting and smelling like a swimming pool.

Contaminants You Can't See

Lead, PFAS, pesticides, and heavy metals are odorless and colorless — yet they're found in many South Florida water supplies.

Unfiltered shower water loaded with chlorine strips your skin's natural oils, leaving skin dry and hair brittle.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does the filtration system actually remove?

Our whole-house filtration systems remove chlorine, chloramines, VOCs, PFAS, heavy metals (lead, mercury, arsenic), sediment, pesticides, and other contaminants. The exact configuration depends on your water test results.

How is this different from a water softener?

A water softener targets hardness minerals (calcium and magnesium). A filtration system targets chemicals and contaminants — chlorine, PFAS, lead, pesticides, and more. Many Palm Beach County homes benefit from both, but they solve different problems.

How long does installation take?

Most whole-house filtration installations take 2–4 hours. Our specialist will complete the work in a single visit, and you'll have clean, filtered water from every tap the same day.

How often do filters need to be replaced?

The main filtration media lasts 3–5 years depending on usage and water quality. Pre-filters (sediment, carbon) are typically replaced every 6–12 months. We include a free annual re-test to monitor performance.
