Carbon Filter Service

Carbon Filter Rebed & Media Replacement

Activated carbon media removes chlorine, chloramines, VOCs, and organic compounds — but it exhausts over time and must be replaced. A depleted carbon bed stops protecting your equipment and starts passing contaminants downstream.

Section Offer
Free 30-Day Post-Service Water Quality Verification
Schedule Carbon Rebed →
Signs Your Carbon Needs Rebed
Chlorine taste or smell returns to water, ice machine output degrades, or it's been over 2–3 years since your last service.
What We Do During a Rebed
Full media dump, tank inspection, new NSF-certified activated carbon loaded, backwash cycle tested, downstream water quality verified. Takes 2–4 hours.
Free 30-Day Verification
After your carbon rebed, we include a free 30-day post-service water quality verification — confirming performance before your 30-day window closes.

Warning Signs Your Carbon Bed is Exhausted

Chlorine taste or odor returning to drinking water, beverages, or ice
Ice machine production drops or ice has an off-taste
More than 2 years since last carbon media replacement
Increased fouling on downstream RO membranes or equipment

Water Softener Service

Softener Resin Replacement & Valve Service

Ion exchange resin is the working core of any water softener. It captures hardness minerals during every cycle — but after 8–15 years (or sooner in high-chlorine water), it degrades and stops exchanging effectively, allowing hard water to pass through untreated.

Section Offer
Free Valve Inspection & Calibration with Every Resin Replacement
Schedule Resin Service →
Signs Resin Needs Replacing
Hard water symptoms return despite regeneration running normally — scale reappears, soap won't lather, and hardness tests positive downstream of the softener.
Our Resin Replacement Process
Full media removal, tank inspection, fresh high-capacity resin loaded, brine tank cleaned, valve inspected and calibrated, regeneration cycle tested. All brands serviced.
Free Valve Inspection Included
Every resin replacement includes a free valve inspection and calibration — ensuring regeneration is programmed correctly for your water hardness and usage patterns.

Warning Signs Your Softener Resin is Depleted

Scale deposits returning on equipment even though the softener is running
Hardness test positive downstream of a running softener
Resin beads visible in water (broken down media escaping the tank)
System is 8+ years old and has never had a resin replacement

Reverse Osmosis Service

Commercial RO Membrane & Filter Service

Commercial RO membranes typically last 2–5 years depending on feed water quality, operating pressure, and pre-treatment effectiveness. Fouled or degraded membranes reduce output pressure, flow rate, and rejection rate — meaning contaminants pass through untreated.

RO Membrane Replacement
We source the correct membrane specification for your system, replace under pressure, and verify rejection rate with a TDS meter before and after service. Documented service record provided.
Filter Stage Replacement
Pre-filter, carbon block, and post-filter cartridge replacement on schedule. Correct pre-filtration is what keeps RO membranes performing at full life — skipping this shortens membrane life significantly.
Scheduled Maintenance Plan
Enroll in our commercial RO maintenance plan and get automatic service scheduling, 10% off all replacement parts, and a documented service history for compliance records.

Warning Signs Your RO System Needs Service

Permeate flow rate has dropped noticeably (system producing less water per hour)
TDS of product water has risen — rejection rate declining
Pre-filters are overdue for replacement (reducing membrane protection)
System is 2+ years old with no documented membrane service

DI Filter Service

Deionization Filter Service & Resin Replacement

Deionization (DI) filters produce ultra-pure, spot-free water critical for car washes, window cleaners, auto detailers, window film installers, and manufacturers who need zero dissolved solids. When DI resin is exhausted, TDS rises and spotting returns — a direct impact on output quality and customer satisfaction.

Section Offer
Free Water Purity Baseline Test with First DI Service Visit
Schedule DI Service →
Car Washes & Detailers
Spot-free rinse arches, hand-wash stations, and detailing water supply all require 0 TDS. When DI is spent, spotting returns and customer complaints follow immediately.
Window Cleaners & Glass Contractors
Water-fed pole systems and glass cleaning applications require 0–10 TDS water to prevent mineral spotting on glass. We service all WFP DI systems and tankless resin housing.
Manufacturers & Labs
Process water and lab applications requiring ultra-pure water. We verify output purity before completing service — your process doesn't restart until water meets your specification.

Warning Signs Your DI Resin is Exhausted

TDS meter reads above 10–20 ppm at output (resin is spent)
Spotting visible on vehicles, glass, or rinsed surfaces
Resin color has shifted from yellow/orange to dark brown or black
Higher water usage than usual (system running longer to compensate)
