Does Your Water Have These Problems?

South Florida water is notoriously hard and full of contaminants. Our free test identifies exactly what's in yours.

Orange & Rust Stains

Iron in your water leaves permanent orange stains in sinks, tubs, and toilets.

Hard Water Scale

White crusty buildup on faucets, shower heads, and inside appliances.

Sulfur / Rotten Egg Smell

Well water in Palm Beach County often smells like rotten eggs, especially when heated.

Dry Skin & Brittle Hair

Hard water strips moisture. Your skin and hair feel rough no matter what products you use.

Bad-Tasting Drinking Water

Chlorine and chemicals from city water make drinking tap water unpleasant.

Appliance Damage

Hard water destroys your water heater and appliances years before they should fail.
