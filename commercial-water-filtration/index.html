Commercial Water System FAQ

Is the commercial water assessment really free?

Yes — completely free and no obligation. We test your water on-site, measure your flow rates and usage patterns, and deliver a written system recommendation report. You get the report whether you hire us or not. Most assessments are completed within the same week you request.

What's the difference between commercial and residential water systems?

Commercial systems are engineered for higher flow rates, continuous duty cycles, and strict compliance requirements that residential systems aren't designed to handle. A restaurant running a dish machine 8 hours a day needs a fundamentally different system than a home — and the consequences of failure are operational, not just inconvenient.

What areas do you serve for commercial work?

We serve all of Martin County and Palm Beach County, Florida — including West Palm Beach, Stuart, Hobe Sound, Jupiter, Palm Beach Gardens, Lake Worth, Boca Raton, and surrounding areas. For large commercial or industrial projects, we extend coverage across South Florida — contact us to confirm availability.

How long does a commercial system installation take?

Installation time depends on system complexity and facility type. Most single-point commercial RO or softener installs are completed in 4–8 hours and scheduled to minimize disruption to your operation. Multi-stage or custom-engineered systems may require phased installation over 1–2 days.

Do you service equipment from other companies?

Yes — we service and maintain all major brands of commercial water treatment equipment, regardless of who installed it. If you have an existing system that needs maintenance, rebed, or repair, visit our commercial maintenance page or call us directly at (561) 309-6270.

What's the ROI on a commercial water filtration system?

ROI varies by application, but commercial water treatment typically pays for itself within 12–36 months. For restaurants, reduced equipment repairs and extended dishwasher/ice machine life are the primary drivers. For multi-family properties, reduced plumbing maintenance and fewer tenant complaints are the main factors. Your free assessment report will include a specific ROI projection for your facility.
